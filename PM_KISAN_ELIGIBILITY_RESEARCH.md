## Status key

- **VERIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCE**: the cited Government of India source was reachable and its relevant text was readable on the check date. This verifies what that source says, not an individual farmer's eligibility.
- **HISTORICAL / CURRENT APPLICABILITY UNVERIFIED**: the official source states the rule, but it is dated, time-limited, or not enough to establish that the rule still applies as written.
- **UNVERIFIED**: the available official page or document did not provide readable evidence for the claim.
- **MANUAL VERIFICATION REQUIRED**: a government record, current state process, or farmer-specific fact is needed.

## Findings by rule

### 1. Who the scheme covers and what “family” means

- **Rule/source summary:** The revised FAQ says the scheme was extended effective 2019-06-01 to all farmer families irrespective of landholding size. The revised 2020 guidelines describe the target as landholding farmer families with cultivable land. They define a farmer family as husband, wife, and minor children who own cultivable land as shown in the concerned State/UT land records.
- **Official sources checked:**  
  https://www.pmkisan.gov.in/Documents/RevisedFAQ.pdf  
  https://www.pmkisan.gov.in/Documents/RevisedPM-KISANOperationalGuidelines(English).pdf  
  https://www.pmkisan.gov.in/
- **Status:** **VERIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCES** for the wording of those documents. **MANUAL VERIFICATION REQUIRED** for an individual family's land, family composition, and current State/UT record.
- **Important limitation:** The 2018 scheme document and older basic FAQ contain an up-to-2-hectare limit. That was the initial rule; the revised FAQ explicitly says the scheme was later extended irrespective of landholding size. Do not encode the old 2-hectare limit as a current PM-KISAN eligibility condition.
- **Confidence:** High that the revised official FAQ and 2020 guidelines state this; medium for applying every detail to a case in 2026 without current authority confirmation.

### 2. Land records, land use, and landholding changes

- **Rule/source summary:** The revised 2020 guidelines say the existing State/UT land-ownership system is used to identify beneficiaries and calculate benefits, and that eligible names are entered in land records, with stated exceptions for North-Eastern States and Jharkhand. The 2019 additional FAQ says urban and rural cultivable land can be covered, urban land must be under actual cultivation, non-cultivable micro-holdings are excluded, and agricultural land used for non-agricultural purposes is not covered.
- **Official sources checked:**  
  https://www.pmkisan.gov.in/Documents/RevisedPM-KISANOperationalGuidelines(English).pdf  
  https://www.pmkisan.gov.in/Documents/ADDITIONAL-FAQ-IN-RESPECTIVE-OF-THE-PM2.pdf
- **Status:** **VERIFIED IN OFFICIAL DOCUMENTS** as published; the additional FAQ is dated 2019-02-13 and current state-specific handling is **HISTORICAL / CURRENT APPLICABILITY UNVERIFIED**.
- **Landholding cut-off caution:** The 2020 guidelines specify 2019-02-01 as a cut-off and describe a five-year period and exceptions, including succession. Those are historical, time-bounded provisions; they do not by themselves establish the rule after that stated period. The current portal homepage (checked 2026-11-19) says cases involving land ownership acquired after 2019-02-01 may be suspected and benefits temporarily withheld pending physical verification. It does **not** say every such case is finally ineligible.
- **Current status:** **MANUAL VERIFICATION REQUIRED** for land acquisition dates, succession, land-record changes, actual cultivation, and State/UT exceptions. The 2026 homepage notice reports temporary holds pending physical verification, not a new complete eligibility test.
- **Confidence:** High for what each dated source says; low for applying the historical cut-off as a current automatic decision.

### 3. Exclusion categories

- **Rule/source summary:** The revised 2020 guidelines list these exclusions:
  1. Institutional landholders.
  2. A farmer family with a member who is or was a constitutional post-holder.
  3. A farmer family with a member who is or was a Minister/State Minister, MP, MLA/MLC, Municipal Corporation Mayor, or District Panchayat Chairperson.
  4. A farmer family with a serving or retired officer/employee of the listed Central/State Government bodies, public-sector enterprises, attached/autonomous government institutions, or regular local-body employees. The guideline excludes Multi Tasking Staff/Class IV/Group D employees from this category.
  5. A farmer family with a superannuated/retired pensioner receiving monthly pension of Rs. 10,000 or more, subject to the stated Multi Tasking Staff/Class IV/Group D exception.
  6. A farmer family with a member who paid income tax in the last assessment year.
  7. A farmer family with a practicing doctor, engineer, lawyer, chartered accountant, or architect registered with the relevant professional body.
  8. For new beneficiaries uploaded to the portal, landholding farmer families who are Non-Resident Indians under the Income Tax Act.
- **Official source checked:** https://www.pmkisan.gov.in/Documents/RevisedPM-KISANOperationalGuidelines(English).pdf (revised 2020-03-29). The portal's readable Scheme Exclusion section also lists categories 1-7: https://www.pmkisan.gov.in/
- **Status:** **VERIFIED IN OFFICIAL DOCUMENTS** for the dated list. Whether a particular person/family falls within a category is **MANUAL VERIFICATION REQUIRED**. The official homepage's static exclusion section is marked last updated 2025-06-20, so confirm current guidance before turning these into automated rules.
- **Confidence:** High for the published categories; medium for current administration and individual application.

### 4. Family-level and multiple-beneficiary cases

- **Rule/source summary:** The guidelines define the family as husband, wife, and minor children. A current homepage notice says the Department has identified “suspected cases” that may fall under exclusions, including land acquired after 2019-02-01 and more than one family member receiving benefits. It says benefits in such cases have been temporarily withheld until physical verification.
- **Official source checked:** https://www.pmkisan.gov.in/ (homepage notice checked 2026-11-19); https://www.pmkisan.gov.in/Documents/RevisedPM-KISANOperationalGuidelines(English).pdf
- **Status:** The family definition is **VERIFIED IN OFFICIAL GUIDELINES**. The current portal notice is **VERIFIED AS A NOTICE ABOUT SUSPECTED CASES AND TEMPORARY HOLDS**, not proof that every multiple-beneficiary or post-cut-off case is finally excluded.
- **Required action:** Do not create a categorical exclusion from the notice alone. Refer these cases to the official Know Your Status channel and State/UT physical verification.
- **Confidence:** High for the notice's wording; low for a final outcome without farmer-specific government verification.

### 5. Exclusion declarations and State/UT verification

- **Rule/source summary:** The revised 2020 guidelines say State/UT Governments may certify exclusion eligibility based on beneficiary self-declaration; when the beneficiary is unavailable, the State/UT may consider a declaration by another adult family member. The guidelines also assign identification of eligible families to State/UT Governments and describe verification using the relevant government and land records.
- **Official source checked:** https://www.pmkisan.gov.in/Documents/RevisedPM-KISANOperationalGuidelines(English).pdf
- **Status:** **VERIFIED IN THE 2020 GUIDELINES**; the currently applicable workflow and declarations must be confirmed with the State/UT.
- **Confidence:** High for the document text; medium for current procedure.

### 6. Registration, identity, bank details, and eKYC

- **Rule/source summary:** The current official registration page visibly asks for Aadhaar number, mobile number, and State. The revised 2020 guidelines describe State/UT preparation of beneficiary records, including identity and bank details, and identity verification through Aadhaar or specified alternatives in the then-described cases. The official homepage checked 2026-11-19 states: “eKYC is MANDATORY for PMKISAN Registered Farmers.” It also says biometric-based eKYC is available for CSC VLE logins, the PM-KISAN mobile application, and State Authorized User logins.
- **Official sources checked:**  
  https://www.pmkisan.gov.in/RegistrationFormupdated.aspx  
  https://www.pmkisan.gov.in/Documents/RevisedPM-KISANOperationalGuidelines(English).pdf  
  https://www.pmkisan.gov.in/  
  https://www.pmkisan.gov.in/KnowAboutEKYC.aspx
- **Status:** The registration fields and homepage eKYC statement are **VERIFIED AS PUBLISHED**. The current full document list, all current identity alternatives, the exact eKYC workflow, and any exceptions are **UNVERIFIED** from the retrieved pages. The 2020 Aadhaar alternatives are dated and should not be presented as current exceptions without a newer official source.
- **Required action:** Treat identity, land-record, bank-account, and eKYC completion as **MANUAL / OFFICIAL-PORTAL VERIFICATION**. Never collect Aadhaar or bank details in this prototype.
- **Confidence:** High for the visible form fields and current homepage statement; low for complete current process details.

### 7. Benefit amount (not an eligibility rule)

- **Rule/source summary:** The official homepage and revised 2020 guidelines describe Rs. 6,000 per year in three equal installments, subject to eligibility/exclusions. The revised FAQ also describes the annual amount and installments.
- **Official sources checked:**  
  https://www.pmkisan.gov.in/  
  https://www.pmkisan.gov.in/Documents/RevisedPM-KISANOperationalGuidelines(English).pdf  
  https://www.pmkisan.gov.in/Documents/RevisedFAQ.pdf
- **Status:** **VERIFIED IN OFFICIAL SOURCES** as the published scheme benefit. The official authority still decides whether any individual payment is due and the current payment/status.
- **Confidence:** High for the scheme-level published amount; no conclusion about an individual beneficiary's payment.

## What cannot be determined from the current Kisan Sahayak farmer profile

The current profile type contains state, district, crops, land size/unit, optional landholding type, irrigation, and language. It does not contain authoritative evidence for:

- Whether the farmer/family is recorded as owning cultivable land in State/UT records, the land record's current status, actual land use, or a succession/transfer history.
- The scheme family unit (spouse and minor children), family-level aggregation, or whether another member already receives benefits.
- Institutional-landholder status.
- Current/former government or constitutional office/employment, pension amount and staff-category exceptions, income-tax filing status, professional registration/practice, or NRI status.
- Aadhaar identity verification, eKYC completion, bank-account seeding, official application/beneficiary status, or physical verification outcome.
- Any State/UT-specific exception or currently applicable administrative workflow.

The profile's landholding type and area are farmer-entered values, not proof from government records. The presence of a crop field does not establish a PM-KISAN crop condition; no crop-specific PM-KISAN rule was found in the official sources reviewed. The checked official sources also did not provide a current, explicit tenant/sharecropper decision rule that would justify automatically declaring such a farmer eligible or ineligible.

## Automation assessment

### Potentially automatable only after authoritative source/data confirmation

- Display official scheme-level facts, with the source date and a clear notice that individual qualification is decided by the government.
- Collect a farmer's self-reported State, land information, and family-related responses for guidance, but label these as unverified inputs.
- If an authorized, current State/UT data integration is later available, compare verified farmer-specific evidence against explicitly verified conditions. Such an integration is not present in the current profile or this research.

### Must remain manual with the current system

- Land-record ownership, land use/cultivation, succession and acquisition history.
- Family composition and duplicate/multiple family beneficiary checks.
- Every exclusion category requiring government, tax, employment, pension, or professional-registration evidence.
- Identity checks, Aadhaar/eKYC, bank details, portal registration, and beneficiary status.
- Cases flagged by the official portal for physical verification and current State/UT exceptions.

No positive or negative PM-KISAN eligibility decision is supportable from the current farmer profile alone.

## Current project-data implications (research only)

- `backend/src/schemes.json` contains PM-KISAN prose, `status: "active"`, and a catalogue verification date of 2026-11-14. It has no structured `conditions` array and no `verification_status` field.
- `backend/src/eligibility.ts` requires a verified scheme and required structured conditions; without them it returns `unknown`. Its supported profile fields do not provide the authoritative family, exclusion, land-record, identity, eKYC, or beneficiary evidence listed above.
- Therefore this research must **not** be translated directly into automatic PM-KISAN eligibility rules. The official source statements and current applicability of dated clauses need human review before any future implementation.

## Recommendation before implementation

Keep PM-KISAN eligibility as **unknown / requiring official verification**. Before adding any structured condition, have a reviewer confirm the currently operative PM-KISAN guidelines and the State/UT-specific process from an official authority, then map only conditions that can be proven from permitted data. Do not use the historical 2-hectare limit, the expired-looking 2019-2024 cut-off wording, a self-reported profile field, or the portal's “suspected cases” notice as a stand-alone automatic eligibility decision.
