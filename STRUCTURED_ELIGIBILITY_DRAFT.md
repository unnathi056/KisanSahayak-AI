**Draft status:** Research-derived proposal; not approved for implementation.

**No application code, Supabase records, verification statuses, or migrations were changed.**

## Draft interpretation rules

- `source_confirmed_pending_review` means only that the research report records an official-source statement. It is a **draft label, not the application's `verified` status**, and must never be mapped to `verified` or treated as verified by the matcher.
- `unknown_source_date` means the research report did not establish a reliable publication/update date. Dates shown below are only those present in that report.
- A condition with `manual_verification_required: true` cannot independently make a farmer Eligible. Profile comparison fields can help route or filter only when the required official scope is recorded and reviewed.
- `matching_method` describes a possible future method, not an active matching rule. Where the research did not state an exact threshold/value, this draft intentionally does not create one.
- `farmer_profile_field: null` means no current profile field can evaluate the condition. For conditions that can only be partially compared, the field is named and its limitation is stated.
- The sections below separate eligibility rules from administrative scope/verification checks. Administrative rows are not eligibility conditions and must not be evaluated as pass/fail eligibility rules.

## Automatic matching suitability

**Suitable only as scoped comparisons after separate source review:**

- PM-KISAN `state` as a routing/scope hint. This is not eligibility.
- PMFBY `state`, `district`, and `crops` against an exact, current Karnataka season notification once notification metadata and values have been independently reviewed and stored. No such condition values are provided in this draft.
- PM-KUSUM `state`/`district` as routing to the SIA/DISCOM only. Component A/B/C eligibility itself is not suitable for automatic matching using current profile fields.
- PM-KISAN land-size comparison is **not suitable now**. It can be considered only after confirming current applicability of any threshold, the exact cultivable land definition, and family pooling; the current individual land-size field does not provide this.

These limited comparisons can return a profile-scope match, never official eligibility approval. As of this draft, none of these condition rows is approved for automatic eligibility decisions or notifications.

## Manual verification required

- PM-KISAN family composition, title/land records, land record date, family-pooled cultivable holding, current threshold applicability, exclusions, eKYC, duplication and State/UT determination.
- PMFBY insurable interest, crop/area/season notification, insured parcel and area, loan status where applicable, State-prescribed tenant/non-loanee documents, enrollment and deadline.
- PM-KUSUM current Karnataka component allocation/window, applicant category, component-specific pump/grid/project/site facts, implementing agency/DISCOM selection, technical conditions, and any applicability of the limited 2026 extension.

## Missing information from the current Farmer Profile

- **PM-KISAN:** family membership and pooled holdings; cultivable land and authoritative land record/date; institutional holding; exclusion-category facts; official State identification and eKYC status. Do not add Aadhaar or sensitive identity document capture.
- **PMFBY:** crop season/year, notification reference, insured plot/area, sowing/enrollment status, insurable-interest evidence, loan/loanee status, and State-approved document status.
- **PM-KUSUM A:** participant organization/category, project capacity/site/land arrangement/grid access and allocation.
- **PM-KUSUM B:** participant category, pump type/capacity/off-grid facts, applicable technical criteria and allocation.
- **PM-KUSUM C:** existing grid-connected pump/feeder/DISCOM details, implementation mode, selection/allocation and project-specific dates.

These gaps do not imply that the farmer is ineligible; they require an Unknown/manual outcome.

## Recommended next implementation steps

1. Have an authorized scheme reviewer confirm each controlling document, amendments, current effective date, Karnataka applicability, and applicable PMFBY notification or PM-KUSUM component status.
2. Decide an explicit condition verification lifecycle. Do not map `source_confirmed_pending_review` or `unknown_*` to application `verified`.
3. Refine this draft into atomic conditions only where an exact requirement and scope are established. Maintain separate records for PM-KUSUM A, B and C.
4. Define condition-level source URL, source/document date, last-verified date, scope (`state`, season/period, component, valid interval), and verification status. Unknown facts remain requirements/manual checks, not pass conditions.
5. Add only minimum, non-sensitive profile fields after a privacy and product review; keep official records, eKYC, loan, and sensitive-document verification outside the automatic matcher.
6. On approval, propose the database schema/migration separately for review. Do not seed real conditions or change PM-KUSUM status before explicit approval.
7. Test that missing, stale, unverified, scope-mismatched, or manually required conditions yield Unknown and cannot generate notifications; test each component separately.

## Information still needed before implementation

- **PM-KISAN:** authorized confirmation of the currently effective operational guideline and amendments; whether the earlier 2-hectare language applies now; precise current land/family definitions and exclusions; and the official State/UT verification workflow. Do not treat the 2-hectare row as an active matcher condition until confirmed.
- **PMFBY:** the current applicable guidelines and exact Karnataka crop/area notification by season; its publication/effective dates, insured units and enrollment dates; and current requirements for insurable interest, loanee coverage and tenant/non-loanee evidence.
- **PM-KUSUM A, B, and C separately:** current MNRE guideline/amendment set, Karnataka component allocation and application availability, SIA/DISCOM process, and component-specific technical/project facts. Confirm the September 2026 memorandum applies only to its described existing projects and limited timeline extension.
- **Data governance:** an authorized condition reviewer, condition-level verification and expiry policy, source/date fields, and a safe process for manual checks that does not collect Aadhaar, bank details, or sensitive identity documents.
- **Matcher design:** decide which verified, scoped facts may be used only for profile matching and which official/manual facts must remain Unknown. Until these questions are resolved and reviewed, no row in this draft should be promoted to application `verified` or used to generate eligibility notifications.
