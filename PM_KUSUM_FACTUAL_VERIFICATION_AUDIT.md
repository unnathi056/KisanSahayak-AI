## A. Audit methodology

1. Reviewed all 30 records in the current structured draft, including the updated Component A PPA record ID `pmkusum-a-ppa-term`.
2. Read the PM-KUSUM structured-draft audit and both PM-KUSUM research documents to preserve their scope cautions and prior source findings.
3. Compared the exact claims to the current browser-rendered MNRE scheme page, the national PM-KUSUM component pages, the MNRE documents listing, and KREDL's Karnataka Solar Water Pumps page. The pages were checked on 19 November 2026.
4. Used the MNRE page and its linked 1 August 2022 extension order to verify the published scheme-period end. Checked document-listing titles and dates for later notices, but did not infer their legal effect from their titles.
5. Used these evidence-status meanings:
   - **VERIFIED:** An official source directly supports the narrow, attributed statement as published. This does not establish that the rule remains applicable to a new project or that applications are open.
   - **NEEDS FURTHER VERIFICATION:** An official source supports part of the statement, but current effect, scope, or a conflict with another official source remains unresolved.
   - **UNVERIFIED:** No reviewed source affirmatively establishes the claimed individual/current rule or status. Absence from a table or summary is not proof of non-availability or ineligibility.
6. Distinguished the source's statement from current legal effect. In particular, an undated webpage labelled “Current Phase” is evidence of what the agency currently publishes, but not, by itself, proof of new application availability or of an overall scheme extension.

No live application submission, farmer record, database, or project allocation was checked. No farmer was assessed.

## C. Record-by-record findings

### Scheme-wide

#### 01. `pmkusum-status-scheme-period`

- **Claim audited:** MNRE states “Till 31.03.2026”; the draft does not claim continuation or enrollment after that date.
- **Official evidence:** S1 states “Till 31.03.2026.” S2 is the 1 August 2022 MNRE order extending the scheme through 31 March 2026.
- **Authority/date/currentness:** Central MNRE; the scheme page is undated, and the extension order is dated 2022-08-01. The stated end date has passed as of this audit.
- **Finding:** Direct support exists for the historical end date. The 2026 document listing includes extension-related titles, but the documents' contents were not readable and do not establish an overall extension.
- **Recommended evidence status:** **NEEDS FURTHER VERIFICATION** for any post-2026-03-31 continuation. The draft's status is correctly cautious.

### Component A — Decentralized grid-connected renewable power plants

#### A-01. `pmkusum-a-beneficiary-categories`

- **Claim audited:** The Component A portal lists individual farmers, farmer groups, cooperatives, panchayats, FPOs, and WUAs.
- **Official evidence:** S3 Component A page lists these categories as eligible beneficiaries; S1 also lists the principal farmer/group entities as renewable power generators.
- **Authority/date/currentness:** Central MNRE/PM-KUSUM portal; no publication/update date on the component summary; checked 2026-11-19.
- **Finding:** Direct support for the listed categories. The pages do not establish that every listed category can participate in every State or project; an applicable EOI may define process and authorization.
- **Recommended evidence status:** **VERIFIED** for the portal-listed categories; current project acceptance remains notice-dependent.

#### A-02. `pmkusum-a-project-capacity`

- **Claim audited:** Component A describes ground- or stilt-mounted grid-connected solar or other renewable plants, 500 kW to 2 MW, on farmers' land.
- **Official evidence:** S3 Component A page directly gives the technology, mounting description, range, and farmer-land wording. S1 confirms the 500 kW–2 MW range.
- **Authority/date/currentness:** Central MNRE/PM-KUSUM portal; undated summaries, checked 2026-11-19.
- **Finding:** Direct support for the scheme/project description. Neither source proves a particular parcel's capacity, title, feasibility, or current project eligibility.
- **Recommended evidence status:** **VERIFIED** as the published project range and description; not as an individual parcel test.

#### A-03. `pmkusum-a-land-siting`

- **Claim audited:** The overview mentions barren/fallow land; the Component A detail page says farmers' land; the sources do not establish a universal mandatory barren/fallow, acreage, title, lease, or siting test.
- **Official evidence:** S3 overview says plants up to 2 MW may be installed on barren/fallow land; S3 Component A page says plants are on farmers' land. Neither page supplies a universal parcel test.
- **Authority/date/currentness:** Central portal; undated summaries, checked 2026-11-19.
- **Finding:** The distinction and absence of a universal test are represented safely. “Barren/fallow” is real overview wording but is not shown as a universal Component A disqualification criterion.
- **Recommended evidence status:** **NEEDS FURTHER VERIFICATION** for any project-specific land-use/title/siting rule. Do not mark a farmer ineligible based on the overview phrase alone.

#### A-04. `pmkusum-a-substation-capacity`

But the KREDL phase extends into 2026-27 while the MNRE period shown ends 2026-03-31. Later 2026 extension-related documents were listed but their text was not readable. Do not treat this as proof of new applications or current legal coverage after March 2026.
- **Recommended evidence status:** **NEEDS FURTHER VERIFICATION** for applying the phase/funding statement to new or post-2026-03-31 cases. The source attribution and arithmetic are verified.

## F. Central versus Karnataka-specific rules and apparent conflicts

### Pump capacity: central summary versus Karnataka KREDL

S1 says Component B supports off-grid standalone pumps **up to 7.5 HP**. S5 says MNRE has permitted Karnataka Component B pumps above 7.5 HP, while restricting central assistance to the subsidy amount for a 7.5-HP pump and assigning additional cost under approved guidelines. These are not safe to reconcile by assuming either a universal 7.5-HP maximum or a universal State exception. Verify the current approval and the exact scope of the Karnataka phase before treating above-7.5-HP pumps as eligible for installation or assistance.

### Scheme period versus KREDL phase dates

S1 and the 1 August 2022 order state a period ending 31 March 2026. S5 labels a Karnataka Component B phase 2024-25 to 2026-27. S4 lists 2026 extension-related documents, but their text was not readable. This is an unresolved timing discrepancy, not proof that all PM-KUSUM components or new farmer applications continued through 2026-27.

### Funding shares

The central MNRE page describes at least a 30% State share and maximum farmer share for the general Component B structure; KREDL states Karnataka's 30% MNRE / 50% State / 20% beneficiary pattern for its named phase. These statements are not numerically contradictory: the Karnataka State share is higher than the general minimum and the beneficiary share lower than the general maximum. The KREDL percentages remain specific to its cited phase and must not be applied to A, C-IPS, C-FLS, or other States.

### Component A tariff wording

The detailed Component A portal says purchase at a pre-fixed levelized tariff for 25 years; the MNRE summary says the FiT is determined by the respective SERC. The 25-year term is directly stated by the portal, but the actual tariff authority and terms must be confirmed from the applicable SERC order, EOI, and PPA.
